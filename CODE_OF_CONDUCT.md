# Code of Conduct

Semantic Detector welcomes respectful, technically grounded participation from researchers, developers, students, and security practitioners.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their technical merits.
- Be specific and reproducible when reporting bugs or experimental results.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Credit prior work, datasets, code, and third-party implementations appropriately.
- Remove credentials, private traffic, personal data, and other sensitive material before posting examples.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, doxxing, credential sharing, or publishing data you are not authorized to disclose are not acceptable in this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these rules and may restrict participation when necessary. Security-sensitive reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of being posted publicly.
