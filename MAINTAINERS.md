# Maintainers

## Primary maintainer

- [@Zhao-create-zc](https://github.com/Zhao-create-zc) — project owner and primary maintainer

The primary maintainer is responsible for release decisions, review of contract-breaking changes, security coordination, third-party license compliance, and the long-term direction of the repository.

## Becoming a maintainer

Consistent contributors may be invited to help maintain a focused area after demonstrating sustained, reviewable contributions. Maintainer access is not granted solely on commit count; contract quality, tests, documentation, reproducibility, and respectful collaboration all matter.

## Decision making

Routine changes are handled through pull-request review. Changes to public data contracts, semantic taxonomy, benchmark methodology, or licensing require explicit maintainer review and corresponding documentation updates.
